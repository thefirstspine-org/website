Get ready for the Early Access of Arena!

Let's get straight to the point: Feb 9th, you will be able to play the demo publicly. On Feb 23rd, we will be present on the Steam Next Fest. Finally, the Early access will land on March 4th!
Wishlist us
Feb 9th: Public demo
Influencers and press will be able to share their first impressions on that day. The demo will be available to everyone on Steam, so don't forget to wishlist the game to get notified when it's out!
The demo will feature two different game modes: beginner and advanced. The beginner mode will allow new players to discover the game mechanics with a guided tutorial, while the advanced mode will let experienced players jump right into the action with more complex strategies.
Get ready and wishlist us
Feb 23rd: Steam Next Fest
During the Steam Next Fest, we will host live streams and Q&A sessions to engage with the community and showcase the game's features. This is a great opportunity for players to ask questions, provide feedback, and get a deeper understanding of what makes Arena unique.
Join us on Twitch
March 4th: Early Access launch
The Early Access launch will mark the beginning of Arena's journey with the community. Players will have access to a substantial portion of the game, allowing them to experience the core gameplay and provide valuable feedback. We are committed to continuously improving the game based on player input and plan to release regular updates throughout the Early Access period.
