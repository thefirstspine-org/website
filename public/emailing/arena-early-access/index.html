A robust Early Access foundation
The Early Access version already includes the core pillars of the game: a complete card collection system, matchmaking and PvP battles, deciphering for spell creation, and a fully functional forge for crafting creatures and artifacts. These systems have been tested and refined, offering a solid foundation for players to explore and shape through feedback. For us, Early Access is a collaborative journey, with a five‑month roadmap dedicated to expanding features, increasing the power cap, and polishing the experience alongside the community.
Monthly updates begin after launch
The Early Access release is only the beginning. The game will receive monthly updates, each expanding the world and deepening the tactical possibilities. The very first update will introduce new quests, a new method of generating cards, and an entirely new location to explore. This early expansion sets the tone for a live game designed to grow continuously, offering fresh challenges and new layers of strategy month after month.
